# V5.40E — Dermatology Intelligence Lab

Build: 2026.08.14-R25E
MTLPI: v2.4E

## Scope
Adds a dedicated Dermatology researcher workspace on top of V5.40D.

- 33 pharmacology classes
- 102 detailed drug/formulation profiles
- Common lenses: acne, eczema/dermatitis, psoriasis, urticaria, rosacea, dermatophytosis, scabies, bacterial skin infection, herpes/zoster, warts, topical-steroid potency, vitiligo, alopecia and melasma.
- Advanced lenses: systemic retinoids, immunomodulators, biologics and JAK inhibitors.
- Sources: AAD guideline resources, ICMR Dermatology STWs, CDC scabies guidance, DailyMed/FDA labeling.

## Safety boundary
Topical steroid potency/site/duration, systemic retinoids, immunosuppressants, JAK inhibitors and biologics are not auto-prescribed. Exact diagnosis, BSA/site, age, pregnancy, infection status, screening and current label/guideline must be verified.
