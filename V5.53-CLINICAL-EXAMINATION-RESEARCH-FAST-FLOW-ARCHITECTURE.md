# Mahamaya Clinic EMR V5.53 — Clinical Examination Research Fast Flow

**Build:** 2026.08.15-R30  
**Instrument/workflow label:** MCE-RP1 v1.0 — Mahamaya Clinical Examination Research Phenotyping / Rapid Protocol  
**Scope:** General Clinical Examination presentation, structured documentation, research metadata, data-quality visibility, and export support.  
**Safety boundary:** clinician-entered observations and explainable decision support only; not an autonomous diagnostic instrument and not a validated severity/quality score.

## Why V5.53 was created

The previous General Examination workspace was clinically rich but required repeated vertical scanning through many status/grade/remarks rows. For routine OPD and research work this could slow documentation and hide abnormal findings among normal/empty rows.

V5.53 keeps the existing V3.00/V3.30 clinical intelligence, oedema, gait, pain, investigation suggestions and bidirectional workflow, but adds a fast research-oriented layer:

1. **FAST mode** — rapid ten-finding core screen with three explicit states: Not assessed / Absent / Present.
2. **STANDARD mode** — rapid screen plus the original finding-specific grades, sites, remarks and expandable phenotype details.
3. **RESEARCH mode** — standard examination plus structured assessor/visit metadata, missingness/limitation documentation, reproducibility fields, documentation-completeness counters and JSON export.
4. **Vitals snapshot** — read-only context from the existing History & Vitals section; it does not create a second source of truth.
5. **General appearance block** — structured condition, consciousness, orientation, posture, cooperation and interaction fields.
6. **Abnormal-only filter** — hides non-present findings from the rapid and detailed view without deleting them.
7. **Clinician-confirmed “Screened absent” action** — requires explicit confirmation and never converts unexamined fields to normal automatically.
8. **“Remaining not assessed” action** — explicitly documents missing/unperformed items rather than silently treating blank as normal.
9. **Next Focused** — routes to Oedema, Gait, Pain or Interpret & Learn depending on entered context.
10. **Save & Next** — uses the existing General Exam → Systemic workflow and existing save function.

## Research/data-quality design

MCE-RP1 stores the raw observation state separately from interpretation. The research panel records:

- visit/research phase;
- examination setting;
- examiner/assessor identifier;
- patient position;
- verification status;
- dataset status;
- study/cohort tag;
- timestamp;
- examination limitations / reason for missingness;
- research/QA notes;
- explicit choice whether identifiers may be included in export.

The displayed percentages are **documentation/assessment completeness only**. They are not diagnostic accuracy, examination quality, disease severity, prognostic probability or a validated research scale.

## Export architecture

The Research & QA panel can copy or download structured JSON containing:

- protocol/build identity;
- pseudonymized subject code by default;
- vital-sign snapshot;
- core physical examination observations;
- general appearance observations;
- oedema/gait/pain structured data;
- research metadata and limitations;
- documentation-completeness fields;
- clinician decision-support output when available.

Patient identifiers are excluded by default. The clinician can explicitly opt in to identifier export when a governed clinical/research workflow requires it.

## Standards/reference rationale

### HL7 FHIR Observation
HL7 FHIR describes Observation as a resource for direct or derived qualitative/quantitative physiological measurements and supports explicit observation status. V5.53 follows this principle by preserving raw finding state and missing/not-assessed status separately from derived interpretation.

Official references:
- https://hl7.org/fhir/observation.html
- https://hl7.org/fhir/observation-profiles.html
- https://hl7.org/fhir/valueset-observation-status.html

### Vital signs as structured observations
FHIR’s Vital Signs profile groups measurements such as heart rate, respiratory rate, blood pressure, temperature, oxygen saturation, height, weight and BMI. V5.53 therefore shows the existing History & Vitals data as a contextual snapshot rather than duplicating editable values inside General Examination.

Official reference:
- https://hl7.org/fhir/vitalsigns.html

### Gait / mobility
The existing Gait Intelligence module retains the Timed Up & Go field. CDC STEADI provides the TUG as a mobility assessment tool and publishes standardized instructions. V5.53 does not invent a new TUG threshold or diagnostic score.

Official reference:
- https://www.cdc.gov/steadi/media/pdfs/steadi-assessment-tug-508.pdf

### Pain measurement
The existing Pain workspace retains NRS 0–10 and VAS. IASP describes NRS and VAS as pain-intensity measures; these scales quantify pain intensity rather than identify etiology. V5.53 therefore keeps intensity separate from pattern, function and clinical interpretation.

Official reference:
- https://www.iasp-pain.org/resources/toolkits/pain-management-center/chapter4/

## Authentication and cloud boundary

V5.53 intentionally does **not** modify:

- `unlockApp()`
- `lockApp()`
- `restoreSession()`
- `savePins()`
- the Firebase module/import/configuration/authentication block
- role logic
- cloud-save semantics

The new release adds only a Clinical Examination CSS/JS workflow layer plus version/PWA cache integration.

## Device design

The rapid screen is five columns on wide desktop, four on smaller desktop, two on tablet and one on narrow mobile. Touch targets grow on mobile. The research grid collapses responsively. The main command bar is sticky on large screens but static on smaller devices to avoid viewport obstruction.

## Explicit limitations

- Static/syntax tests do not replace live clinician regression on phone, tablet, laptop and desktop.
- “Not assessed” is not equivalent to normal/absent.
- Bulk absent entry is permitted only after explicit clinician confirmation that the listed findings were actually screened.
- General appearance priority flags are context prompts, not diagnoses.
- Pseudonymization is not the same as anonymization; governed research datasets still require applicable privacy, ethics and data-management controls.
- Research use requires the appropriate protocol, ethics/consent, source verification and statistical analysis plan.
