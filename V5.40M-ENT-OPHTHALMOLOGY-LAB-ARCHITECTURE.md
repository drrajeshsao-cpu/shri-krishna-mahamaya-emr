Build: 2026.08.14-R25M

## Scope
Adds a dedicated ENT + eye pharmacology workspace on top of V5.40L.

- 35 pharmacology classes
- 83 detailed drug/formulation profiles
- Ear: cerumen, otitis externa/media, vertigo/BPPV symptom medicines, Ménière context, sudden hearing loss
- Nose/throat: allergic rhinitis, sinusitis, decongestants, pharyngitis and epistaxis adjuncts
- Eye: dry eye, allergic/bacterial conjunctivitis, blepharitis, ocular anti-inflammatory therapy
- Glaucoma: prostaglandins, beta-blockers, alpha2 agonists, CAIs, ROCK inhibitor, miotics and acute angle-closure emergency pharmacology
- Diagnostic mydriatics/cycloplegics, intravitreal anti-VEGF and ocular antiviral research pathways

## Safety boundary
Painful red eye, photophobia, visual loss, corneal findings, chemical injury, ocular trauma, acute angle closure, contact-lens keratitis and sudden sensorineural hearing loss are urgent specialist/emergency pathways. Topical ocular steroids and intravitreal therapies are never routine automatic drafts.
