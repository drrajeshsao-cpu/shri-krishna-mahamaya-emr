# V5.40L — Pediatrics • Child Health Pharmacology Intelligence Lab

Build: 2026.08.14-R25L
MTLPI: v2.4L

## Scope
Adds a pediatric age/weight/formulation-aware pharmacology workspace on top of V5.40K.

- 32 pediatric pharmacology classes
- 55 detailed medicine/formulation/safety profiles
- Fever/pain, asthma/wheeze, allergy/anaphylaxis
- ORS + zinc diarrhea workflow, vomiting and constipation
- Pediatric oral/parenteral antibiotic class learning with stewardship guardrails
- Deworming/scabies, seizure rescue/maintenance, iron/vitamin-D and eczema topical therapy
- Age-band and liquid-formulation safety lenses
- Sources: WHO EMLc 2025, WHO IMCI, ICMR Pediatrics STWs, WHO diarrhea/iron, GINA 2026, NICE fever/constipation, CDC anaphylaxis, DailyMed

## Safety boundary
No adult-to-child dose conversion. Exact dose requires current weight, age, indication, formulation concentration, organ function and a current pediatric label/guideline. Neonatal/critical-care regimens remain separate.
