# Mahamaya Clinic EMR V4.10 — Ayurveda Safety & Selection Navigator

Build: 2026.08.14-R17
MATLI: v1.3

## Core upgrade
V4.10 adds a conservative **Clinical Safety & Selection Matrix** on top of V4.01 formulary discovery. The aim is to help a clinician/researcher compare options without turning incomplete evidence into an automated prescription.

### 1. Evidence hierarchy and selection tiers
- **E1** — curated formulation linked to condition-specific government/institutional clinical evidence in the local MATLI dataset.
- **E2** — curated formulation / official-classical source layer without a condition-specific protocol embedded for the current search.
- **D** — AFI discovery record only.
- “Drug of Choice”, “first-line” and “preferred” are not displayed unless an explicit authoritative guideline supports that wording.

### 2. Situation-aware research lens
Research search can now be viewed through: pediatric, pregnancy, lactation, elderly/frail, renal, hepatic, GI/ulcer, diabetes/metabolic, and polypharmacy/interaction contexts. The engine **re-orders and flags** results conservatively. Unknown safety is never converted to “safe.”

### 3. Special Population & Safety Matrix
Each opened record shows pregnancy/conception, lactation, pediatric/age, elderly/frailty, renal, hepatic, GI/ulcer, diabetes/sugar-load, interaction/polypharmacy and duration/long-term status.

### 4. Dose / administration discipline
Dose, age band, anupana, kala, frequency, duration and maximum-duration fields remain source-gated. AFI discovery records never auto-invent dose. A condition-specific study regimen may be displayed only as an explicitly labelled **Protocol example — not universal dose**.

### 5. Official source enrichment
Source centre now includes PCIM&H, Ministry of Ayush, CCRAS, Ayush Suraksha, AIIA, NIA Drug Discovery & Development, NIA Research Resources, AIIMS New Delhi CIMR, AIIMS Rishikesh AYUSH/Integrative Medicine, e-Charak and e-Samhita/Ayusoft.

### 6. Herbo-mineral safety
Rasaushadhi/Rasayoga/Bhasma/Lauha/Mandura/Parpati/Pishti/Kupipakva remain high-caution classes. Pregnancy, pediatric, renal, hepatic and polypharmacy contexts trigger stronger review language; exact composition, processing, quality, dose and monitoring must be checked against authoritative sources.

## Scientific boundary
This is clinician/research decision support. A traditional indication, AFI index match, single clinical study or absence of a warning does not prove universal efficacy or safety. Patient-specific clinical judgment and source verification remain mandatory.
