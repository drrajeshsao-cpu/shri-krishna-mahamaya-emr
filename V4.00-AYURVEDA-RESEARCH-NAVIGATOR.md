# Mahamaya Clinic EMR V4.00 — Ayurveda Research Navigator

Build: 2026.08.14-R15
MATLI: v1.2

## What changed
- Learning Assist can be switched ON/OFF without replacing the standard prescription workflow.
- Ayurveda condition/research contexts expanded from 24 to 39.
- Complaint-to-Ayurveda bridge broadened for common respiratory, GI, neuro, spine/MSK, GU, dermatology and women’s-health presentations.
- Discovery-only contexts do not create an automatic prescription.
- AFI formulary discovery remains separate from curated MATLI monographs.
- High-caution Rasaushadhi/Bhasma/Lauha/Mandura discovery is excluded from diagnosis-linked lists by default and requires explicit opt-in.
- Search now supports formulation class, source layer and risk filtering.

## Safety design
Complaint ≠ diagnosis. Indexed therapeutic-use wording ≠ patient-specific indication. Dose/anupana/kala/duration are not auto-filled from discovery records. High-caution formulations require explicit verification of exact formulation version, processing, composition, quality, pregnancy/pediatric context, renal/hepatic context, interactions and monitoring.

## Recommended source hierarchy
1. Ministry of Ayush / PCIM&H official AFI/API / monograph ecosystem
2. CCRAS clinical/pharmaceutical/pharmacological research and disease protocols
3. Ayush Suraksha pharmacovigilance
4. Licensed product label and current applicable clinical guidance
