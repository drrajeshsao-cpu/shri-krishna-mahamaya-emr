# Mahamaya Clinic EMR V2.60 — Clinical Workflow Edition

Build: 2026-08-13

## Why this rebuild
V2.60 reorganizes the encounter around clinical reasoning rather than a random list of modules. The home screen now groups the work into Patient & Visit → Complaints/HPI → Examination → Assessment & Diagnosis → Investigations → Ayurveda Assessment → Plan/Safety/PDF.

## Terminology & complaint entry
- Search-as-you-type complaint box; free typing remains allowed.
- Multi-complaint list with primary complaint selection.
- 622 starter searchable terms across symptoms, common conditions and Ayurveda terminology.
- Common abbreviations and selected Hindi aliases for quick search.
- Custom complaint/diagnosis terms can be saved locally to “My Terms”.
- Complaint and diagnosis are kept as separate clinical concepts.
- No fabricated ICD/SNOMED codes are assigned.

## Assessment & Diagnosis
- New dedicated Assessment & Dx step before Investigations and Prescription.
- Search condition/Ayurveda terms; add as Provisional, Differential or Final.
- Manual text remains fully editable.
- Complaint-linked rule prompts can be copied into DDx for clinician review.

## Safety & workflow
- Safety remains before final preview/PDF.
- CBC Interpreter is optional, not a mandatory step for every patient.
- Red flags override routine flow.
- Fixed Firebase-auth → local role bridge so the top role badge can show Doctor after authenticated login.

## Design/reference review
Patterns reviewed from Epic, Oracle Health, athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, DrChrono, Elation, Jane, Cliniko, OpenEMR, Bahmni, CharmHealth, Canvas Medical, Practice Fusion, Tebra, NextGen, OpenMRS, WHO ICD-11, SNOMED CT, Ministry of Ayush terminology resources and AYUSH clinical repositories.

The software remains clinician-operated decision support. It does not autonomously diagnose, prescribe, or replace clinical judgment.
