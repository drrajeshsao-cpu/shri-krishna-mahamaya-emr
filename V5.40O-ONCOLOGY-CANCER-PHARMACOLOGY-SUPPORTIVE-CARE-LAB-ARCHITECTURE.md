# V5.40O — Oncology • Cancer Pharmacology • Supportive Care Intelligence Lab

Build: **V5.40O • MTLPI v2.4O • ONC v1.0 • R25O**

## Scope
A specialist-oriented research workspace for classic cytotoxic chemotherapy classes, biomarker-driven targeted therapy, immune checkpoint/cellular therapy, endocrine oncology and core supportive-care pharmacology.

## Safety boundary
This is **not** a chemotherapy order set, regimen recommender or dose calculator. Cancer treatment requires diagnosis/stage/biomarker confirmation, current protocol, body-size and organ-function assessment, interaction review, hazardous-drug handling and oncology supervision.
