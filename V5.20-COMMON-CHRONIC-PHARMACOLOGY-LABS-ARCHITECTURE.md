## Scientific boundary
These are research/learning pharmacology maps. Context tags are not automatic first-line/Drug-of-Choice rankings. Dose examples are not patient-specific prescriptions. Exact diagnosis, formulation, current label/guideline, organ function, age/weight, pregnancy/lactation, interactions and monitoring require verification.
