# V5.40Q — Tuberculosis • Leprosy • Mycobacterial Intelligence Lab

Build: **V5.40Q • MTLPI v2.4Q • MYCO v1.0 • R25Q**

## Scope
Drug-susceptible TB, TB preventive treatment, MDR/RR-TB and modern all-oral regimens, key anti-TB toxicity/interaction gates, common NTM pulmonary disease (MAC, M. abscessus, M. kansasii), WHO leprosy MDT, lepra reactions/neuritis and contact SDR-PEP.

## Dataset
- 31 pharmacology/regimen classes
- 53 medicine/regimen profiles
- Cumulative: 23 specialized labs • 692 classes • 1656 profiles

## Safety architecture
No automatic regimen generation. Active TB requires diagnostic confirmation and current NTEP/WHO regimen/DST review. DR-TB/NTM are specialist-programme domains. Leprosy requires classification, nerve/eye assessment and reaction surveillance.

## Primary sources
WHO TB Module 4 (2025), WHO TPT Module 1 (2024), India NTEP guidelines and 2025 DR-TB guidance, ATS/ERS/ESCMID/IDSA NTM guideline, WHO leprosy diagnosis/treatment/prevention and reaction/PEP guidance.
