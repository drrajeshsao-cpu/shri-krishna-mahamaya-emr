## Purpose
Dedicated researcher/practitioner pharmacology workspace for common and high-impact cardiovascular and vascular drug domains without duplicating the full EMR workflow.

## Research flow
Antithrombotic / lipid / coronary / HF / electrophysiology / vascular question → class → mechanism/target → generic/formulation → adult learning-dose snapshot → monitoring pearl → source verification.

## Classification lenses
- Antiplatelets, anticoagulants, fibrinolytics, VTE/PE, stroke prevention
- ACS/PCI, chronic angina, anti-ischemic drugs
- Statins and nonstatin lipid-lowering drugs
- HFrEF four pillars, congestion/diuretics, advanced HF
- AF: stroke prevention, rate control, rhythm control
- SVT and ventricular antiarrhythmics
- PAD/claudication and systemic vascular protection
- Pulmonary vascular / PAH drugs
- Emergency / IV / specialist-monitored drugs

## Scientific boundaries
Class/context tags are not automatic prescribing rankings. Fibrinolytics, IV antiarrhythmics/inotropes and PAH-specific therapies require strict indication, contraindication, monitoring and specialist/emergency protocols. Antiplatelet + anticoagulant combinations substantially increase bleeding and must be indication/duration specific.

## Primary source families
2025 ACC/AHA ACS; 2023 ACC/AHA AF; 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA HF; 2023 chronic coronary disease; 2024 ACC/AHA PAD; 2026 ACC/AHA dyslipidemia; 2026 AHA/ACC acute PE; 2024 ESC peripheral arterial/aortic disease; ICMR Cardiology STWs; DailyMed current labeling.
