# Mahamaya EMR V5.41B — Medication Reconciliation • LASA • Dispensing • Storage • Barcode • Medication Error Prevention Intelligence Lab

**Build:** 2026.08.15-R26B  
**Dataset:** MTLPI 2.5B

## Purpose
A clinician/pharmacy medication-safety workspace spanning the full medication-use transition: Best Possible Medication History (BPMH), admission/transfer/discharge reconciliation, discrepancy resolution, patient communication, LASA and labeling safeguards, dispensing/storage controls, barcode verification, traceability, downtime recovery and near-miss/error learning.

## Core safety architecture
1. **BPMH first:** medication history is actively assembled and cross-checked rather than copied forward.
2. **Discrepancy ≠ error:** determine whether a difference is intentional, unintended or unresolved, then document the action.
3. **Every transition matters:** admission, transfer, procedural holds/restarts and discharge all receive reconciliation prompts.
4. **High-alert priority:** anticoagulants, insulin, opioids/sedatives and other high-alert medicines receive prioritized last-dose/intent review.
5. **One patient-facing list:** discharge closure distinguishes continue, stop, change, new and restart plans.
6. **LASA is system-level:** Tall Man lettering, label design, storage segregation, barcode and independent identifiers are complementary barriers.
7. **Barcode/ADC ≠ authorization:** successful scan or cabinet selection does not prove clinical appropriateness.
8. **Near misses are learning signals:** intercepted hazards and downtime workarounds feed closed-loop safety review.

## Scientific boundary
No automatic start, stop, restart, substitution, dispensing, administration, barcode override or medication-error verdict is generated. Verify the patient, exact product/order, medication-history sources, intent of changes, current labeling/formulary, organ function, allergy/ADR history and local pharmacy/medication-safety policy.
