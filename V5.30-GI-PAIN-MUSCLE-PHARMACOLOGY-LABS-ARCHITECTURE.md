## Critical classification boundaries
- Corticosteroids are anti-inflammatory pain-modifiers, not universal analgesics.
- Skeletal muscle spasm, neurologic spasticity and visceral smooth-muscle spasm are separate pharmacologic problems.
- Neuromuscular blockers cause paralysis and provide NO analgesia or sedation.
- Local-anesthetic dosing is procedure/concentration/total-mg specific; LAST preparedness is mandatory.
- H. pylori regimens must follow current resistance/susceptibility and test-of-cure guidance.
