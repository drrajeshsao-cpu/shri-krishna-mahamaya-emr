# V5.40J — Women’s Health • Gynecology Pharmacology Intelligence Lab

Build: 2026.08.14-R25J
MTLPI: v2.4J

## Scope
Adds a dedicated women’s-health / gynecology pharmacology research workspace on top of V5.40I.

- 29 pharmacology/device classes
- 69 detailed drug/formulation/device profiles
- Contraception: CHC, progestin-only, DMPA, implant, LNG-IUS, copper IUD, emergency contraception
- HMB/AUB/dysmenorrhea/fibroid symptom pharmacology
- PCOS: metabolic, cycle/hyperandrogenism and fertility pathways
- Endometriosis: progestins, GnRH agonists/antagonists
- Fertility: letrozole, clomiphene, gonadotropins, triggers, antagonists, luteal progesterone
- Menopause/GSM: systemic/local hormone therapy and nonhormonal VMS drugs
- Pregnancy nausea learning layer and PPH uterotonic/TXA emergency layer
- Sources: CDC US MEC/SPR 2024, NICE HMB NG88, ASRM PCOS 2023, ESHRE endometriosis 2022, NICE menopause, Menopause Society, ASRM fertility/OHSS, WHO PPH 2025, DailyMed

## Safety boundary
Hormonal contraception requires medical-eligibility screening. Fertility drugs and PPH medicines are specialist/emergency protocol therapies; no automatic patient-specific regimen is generated.
