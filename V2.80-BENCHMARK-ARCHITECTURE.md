# V2.80 Benchmark Architecture — Mahamaya Clinic EMR

This benchmark is a design/architecture review of 20 leading medical, practice-management, open-source EMR and Indian/Ayurveda digital-health resources. It is not a claim that every product is clinically equivalent or that Mahamaya Clinic EMR is certified to the same standards.

## V2.80 design decisions derived from recurring patterns

- Active patient context remains visible while navigating modules.
- One logical encounter path with a single “continue” action.
- Search-first patient and terminology entry instead of large fixed dropdown-only workflows.
- Structured fields coexist with free text rather than forcing one documentation style.
- Mobile is a working clinical interface, not merely a read-only copy of desktop.
- Tablet gets its own density/layout behavior rather than inheriting phone or desktop blindly.
- Secondary tools are moved to drawers/panels to reduce top-level visual noise.
- Labs/investigations remain within the patient workflow; SLAIE is an optional diagnostic tool rather than a mandatory step.
- Ayurveda remains a first-class clinical module but long technical/admin groups are kept behind explicit sub-navigation.
- Prescription finalization is sequenced after assessment/investigations and before/with safety review.
- Local save and cloud save are shown separately to avoid a false “synced” impression.
- PWA installation, touch targets, offline app-shell caching and responsive controls are retained across devices.

## What V2.80 deliberately does NOT claim

- It is not a certified EHR/HIS.
- It does not claim HIPAA/DPDP/ABDM compliance merely because Firebase or HTTPS is used.
- It does not replace clinician judgment or original laboratory reports.
- It does not guarantee cloud security without verified Firestore rules, least-privilege roles, backup/restore testing and operational privacy policies.
