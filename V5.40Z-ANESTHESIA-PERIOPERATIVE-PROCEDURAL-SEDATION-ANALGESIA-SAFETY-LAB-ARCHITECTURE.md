# Mahamaya EMR V5.40Z — Anesthesia • Perioperative • Procedural Sedation • Analgesia Safety Intelligence Lab

**Build:** 2026.08.15-R25Z  
**Engine:** MTLPI v2.4Z / Anesthesia & Perioperative Safety Lab v1.0

## Purpose
A clinician-reviewed perioperative workspace for pre-anesthetic assessment, airway/aspiration risk, physiologic monitoring, procedural-sedation rescue, regional/neuraxial safeguards, acute analgesia, PACU recovery and high-risk anesthesia emergencies. It is not an autonomous anesthesia-planning or dose-calculation system.

- 32 total specialized pharmacology labs
- 1053 total specialized classes

## Core workflows
1. Preprocedure patient + procedure + urgency + medication/allergy review.
2. Airway and aspiration/fasting assessment with difficult-airway rescue plan.
3. Monitoring matched to sedation/anesthesia depth and clinical risk.
4. Sedation-continuum awareness with immediate rescue for deeper-than-intended sedation.
5. Regional/neuraxial antithrombotic and cumulative local-anesthetic safety review.
6. LAST, anaphylaxis and malignant-hyperthermia emergency pathway recognition.
7. Multimodal analgesia with opioid/OSA/frailty safety review.
8. Structured PACU recovery and discharge readiness; no elapsed-time-only clearance.

## Safety boundaries
- No automatic anesthetic, sedative, opioid, reversal, local-anesthetic or emergency drug dose.
- No automatic fasting clearance, airway-technique selection or anesthesia fitness declaration.
- No universal antithrombotic hold/restart or neuraxial timing rule.
- No automatic PACU/sedation discharge clearance.
- LAST and malignant-hyperthermia cards point to current ASRA/MHAUS crisis guidance rather than generating crisis doses.
- Current local facility policy and qualified anesthesia clinician judgment remain authoritative.

## Source backbone
ASA preoperative fasting, sedation-continuum, basic monitoring, postanesthesia care, difficult-airway and ASA Physical Status resources; current multi-society GLP-1 perioperative guidance; ASRA LAST and antithrombotic/regional-anesthesia guidance; MHAUS malignant-hyperthermia crisis resources; current product labeling.
