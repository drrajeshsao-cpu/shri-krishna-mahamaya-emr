# V5.40I — Fluids • Electrolytes • Nutrition Intelligence Lab

Build: 2026.08.14-R25I
MTLPI: v2.4I

## Scope
Adds a dedicated fluids/electrolytes/nutrition research workspace on top of V5.40H.

- 24 fluid/electrolyte/nutrition classes
- 44 formulation/component profiles
- Oral rehydration and IV crystalloid strategy
- Sodium/water balance: hypertonic saline, SIADH, DI/free-water concepts
- Potassium, magnesium, calcium and phosphate replacement
- Bicarbonate/acid-base support and albumin
- Refeeding-risk thiamine/electrolyte framework
- Oral nutrition supplements, enteral nutrition and parenteral nutrition macronutrients/micronutrients
- Sources: NICE IV Fluids, WHO ORS, KDIGO, ESE hyponatremia, ASPEN and DailyMed

## Safety boundary
No automatic IV-fluid rate, electrolyte-correction rate or parenteral-nutrition prescription is generated. Severe electrolyte disorders and PN require monitored, patient-specific protocols.
