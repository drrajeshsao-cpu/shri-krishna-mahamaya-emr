# V5.40U — Pregnancy • Lactation • Reproductive Safety Intelligence Lab

Build: **V5.40U • MTLPI v2.4U • REPRO-SAFE v1.0 • 2026.08.14-R25U**

## Purpose
Cross-cutting reproductive medication-safety workspace for preconception, pregnancy, postpartum, breastfeeding and female/male reproductive potential. It is a research/learning CDS layer, not an automatic safe/unsafe classifier.

## Core architecture
- FDA PLLR narrative-risk framework; obsolete A/B/C/D/X letter categories are not used.
- Preconception medication reconciliation and unplanned-exposure review.
- Gestational-age / trimester lens and peripartum-neonatal lens.
- LactMed-centered breastfeeding evidence review and infant-monitoring context.
- Product-specific pregnancy testing, contraception, fertility and washout verification.
- High-risk gates for valproate, systemic retinoids, methotrexate, mycophenolate, RAAS blockers, selected oncology drugs and radiopharmaceuticals.
- Specialty cross-links: cardiovascular, anticoagulation, neuro-psych, diabetes, thyroid, anti-infectives, TB, respiratory, GI, immune-modulators, vaccines and oncology.
- Male reproductive-potential and fertility-preservation layer.

## Safety boundary
Never start, stop, switch, taper or continue a medicine solely from this database. Pregnancy does not erase the risk of untreated maternal disease. Verify current product labeling, exact indication, gestational/postpartum timing, organ function, fetal/infant factors and specialist guidance.

## Authoritative source stack
FDA PLLR and current prescribing information; NIH/NLM LactMed; CDC pregnancy/breastfeeding vaccination resources; drug-specific FDA safety communications/REMS where relevant.
