## Purpose
Scientist-style learning workspace for common kidney and urology pharmacology, layered cumulatively over V5.40C-2.

## Coverage
- CKD/cardiorenal protection: RAAS blockade, SGLT2 inhibitors, finerenone context.
- Volume/electrolytes: loop/thiazide/MRA/ENaC diuretics, potassium binders, alkali therapy.
- CKD complications: phosphate binders, active vitamin-D/calcimimetic therapy, iron/ESA/HIF-PHI anemia pharmacology.
- LUTS/BPH: alpha blockers, 5-alpha-reductase inhibitors, tadalafil context.
- OAB: antimuscarinics and beta-3 agonists; retention/cognitive/BP cautions.
- Stones: citrate, thiazide, urate-lowering, cystinuria-directed therapy, selected medical expulsive context.
- ED/local urology: PDE5 inhibitors, alprostadil, short-term urinary analgesia.

## High-consequence safeguards
- Severe hyperkalemia/hyponatremia/AKI and infected obstruction are emergency/specialist contexts.
- PDE5 inhibitors must not be combined with nitrates.
- BPH/OAB drugs require retention/PVR and differential-diagnosis awareness.
- Stone prevention is phenotype-based; not every stone patient needs the same drug.
- Renal-dose examples must be verified against current indication-specific label and kidney function.

## Primary source hierarchy
KDIGO 2024 CKD, KDIGO 2026 Anemia in CKD, KDIGO CKD-MBD, AUA 2026 BPH, AUA/SUFU 2024 OAB, AUA kidney-stone medical management, AUA ED, ICMR Nephrology/Urology STWs, and current DailyMed labels.
