## Purpose
Dedicated scientist-style learning workspace for bone-metabolism, osteoporosis and common rheumatology pharmacology, layered cumulatively over V5.40C-1.

## Coverage
- Bone health: calcium, vitamin D, nutritional vs active vitamin-D distinction.
- Osteoporosis: oral/IV bisphosphonates, denosumab, PTH/PTHrP anabolics, romosozumab, SERM, calcitonin.
- Sequencing: denosumab/PTH/romosozumab transition lens and very-high-fracture-risk lens.
- Glucocorticoid-induced osteoporosis: risk-stratified treatment/sequence lens.
- Osteoarthritis: symptom pharmacology cross-reference (topical/oral NSAID, acetaminophen, duloxetine, intra-articular steroid) with non-drug-care boundary.
- Gout: acute anti-inflammatory therapy, xanthine-oxidase inhibitors, uricosuric and uricase therapy.
- RA/PsA/SpA/SLE: csDMARDs, TNF/IL-6/B-cell/T-cell/IL-17/IL-23 biologics, JAK inhibitors and selected SLE immunosuppressants/biologics.
- Monitoring lenses: TB/HBV/infection screen, reproductive safety, CBC/LFT/renal monitoring.

## High-consequence safeguards
- Methotrexate in rheumatic disease is generally ONCE WEEKLY, not daily.
- Denosumab should not be stopped without a planned antiresorptive transition where clinically appropriate because rebound bone loss/vertebral fracture risk can occur.
- JAK inhibitor selection requires current boxed-warning risk review (MACE, malignancy, thrombosis, serious infection/mortality).
- Biologic/JAK/potent immunosuppression needs infection/TB/hepatitis/vaccine review.
- Osteoporosis therapy depends on fracture risk, renal function, calcium/vitamin-D status and treatment sequence.

## Primary source hierarchy
ACR clinical practice guidelines (2021 RA, 2020 gout, 2019 OA, 2022 GIOP), Endocrine Society postmenopausal osteoporosis guideline/update, ICMR Fragility Fracture STW, FDA JAK safety communication and current DailyMed labels.
