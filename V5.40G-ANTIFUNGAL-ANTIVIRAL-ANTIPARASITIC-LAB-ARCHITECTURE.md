# V5.40G — Antifungal • Antiviral • Antiparasitic Intelligence Lab

Build: 2026.08.14-R25G
MTLPI: v2.4G

## Scope
Adds a dedicated non-bacterial anti-infective pharmacology workspace on top of V5.40F.

- 34 pharmacology classes
- 97 detailed drug/formulation profiles
- Antifungal: topical/systemic azoles, allylamines, polyenes, echinocandins, mold-active triazoles and niche/newer agents.
- Antiviral: HSV/VZV, influenza, COVID-19, CMV, HBV, HCV and HIV ART classes.
- Antiparasitic: deworming/helminths, Strongyloides, schistosomiasis, intestinal protozoa, malaria, leishmaniasis and toxoplasmosis.
- Source stack: CDC, WHO, NIH/HHS HIV, AASLD-IDSA HCV Guidance, IDSA fungal guidelines and DailyMed/FDA labels.

## Safety boundary
This is a learning/research classification system. Regimen-based infections (HIV/HBV/HCV), invasive fungal disease, severe malaria and complex parasitoses require exact diagnosis, guideline, resistance/susceptibility and specialist/context verification.
