# V5.40T — Geriatrics • Polypharmacy • Deprescribing • Frailty Intelligence Lab

Build: **V5.40T • MTLPI v2.4T • GERI-SAFE v1.0 • R25T**

## Scope
Older-adult medication reconciliation, PIM/PPO screening concepts, anticholinergic/CNS/fall burden, renal-function and hypoglycemia safety, prescribing cascades, treatment burden, frailty-aware goals and supervised deprescribing pathways.

## Dataset
- 37 pharmacology/safety classes
- 69 medicine/strategy profiles
- Cumulative: 26 specialized labs • 796 classes • 1847 profiles

## Safety architecture
No medicine is automatically stopped because a patient is older, frail, taking many medicines or because a drug appears in Beers/STOPP. The lab does not calculate anticholinergic scores, renal doses or taper schedules. Every change requires indication, current dose/duration, withdrawal risk, organ function, cognition/falls, goals and monitoring review.

## Primary sources
- 2023 AGS Beers Criteria and AGS 2025 alternatives resource
- STOPP/START version 3 (2023)
- WHO Medication Safety in Polypharmacy
- NICE NG56 Multimorbidity and NG5 Medicines Optimisation
- Deprescribing.org evidence-based algorithms
- STOPPFrail and BNF/NICE prescribing-in-elderly guidance
