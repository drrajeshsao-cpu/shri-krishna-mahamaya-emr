## Safety architecture
Transplant/immunosuppression research and learning CDS only. This lab is not a transplant regimen generator, rejection-treatment order set, therapeutic-drug-monitoring calculator or infection-prophylaxis autoprescriber. Organ type, time from transplant, donor/recipient serology, immunologic risk, biopsy/DSA findings, organ function, drug concentrations, interacting medicines, pregnancy plans, infection status and the transplant-center protocol must be verified.
