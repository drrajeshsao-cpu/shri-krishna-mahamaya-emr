# V5.40N — Endocrinology • Hormone • Metabolic Intelligence Lab

Build: 2026.08.14-R25N
MTLPI: v2.4N

## Scope
Adds a dedicated endocrine pharmacology workspace on top of V5.40M. Diabetes and thyroid remain in their dedicated earlier labs.

- 33 pharmacology classes
- 55 detailed drug/formulation profiles
- Adrenal insufficiency, adrenal crisis, glucocorticoid withdrawal/HPA recovery
- Primary aldosteronism, Cushing syndrome and pheochromocytoma/PPGL pharmacology
- Hyperprolactinemia/prolactinoma, acromegaly, hypopituitarism, GH deficiency and AVP deficiency
- Confirmed male hypogonadism and fertility-directed gonadotropin therapy
- Chronic obesity pharmacotherapy including GLP-1, dual GIP/GLP-1 and non-incretin options
- Hypoparathyroidism/hyperparathyroidism and central precocious puberty pathways

## Safety boundary
Research/learning CDS only. Endocrine therapy is diagnosis- and lab-dependent. Adrenal crisis is an emergency. PPGL requires sequencing safeguards. Testosterone is not a nonspecific anti-ageing drug and can suppress fertility. Obesity medicines require product-label eligibility and contraindication screening. Pituitary/adrenal tumor therapies and PTH replacement remain specialist-controlled.
