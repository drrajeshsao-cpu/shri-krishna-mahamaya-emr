## Purpose
A rapid clinician-reviewed toxicology workspace for emergency exposure reconstruction, toxidrome recognition, ECG/acid-base interpretation, selective decontamination, antidote verification, extracorporeal-treatment escalation and India/SE-Asia-relevant poisoning patterns. It is not an autonomous poison-identification or dosing system.

## Core workflows
1. ABC + glucose + temperature + seizure/dysrhythmia stabilization.
2. Exact product/formulation/amount/time/route/co-exposure reconstruction.
3. Toxidrome + ECG/QRS/QT + acid-base pattern review.
4. Selective decontamination only when agent/timing/airway/GI context supports it.
5. Antidote review with current source verification; no auto-dose.
6. EXTRIP / dialysis escalation when toxin-specific criteria may be met.
7. Serial reassessment before disposition; no automatic discharge clearance.

## High-yield poison groups
Paracetamol/acetaminophen, salicylates, opioids, benzodiazepines, TCAs, beta-blockers, calcium-channel blockers, digoxin, clonidine, organophosphate/carbamate pesticides, aluminum phosphide, anticoagulant rodenticides, methanol, ethylene glycol, cyanide, carbon monoxide, methemoglobinemia, iron, lead, arsenic, lithium, valproate, isoniazid, theophylline, LAST, sulfonylurea/insulin, hydrocarbons, caustics, amatoxin mushrooms, snakebite and scorpion envenomation.

## Safety boundaries
- No automatic antidote dose, infusion, repeat-dose rule or decontamination order.
- No routine activated charcoal / gastric-emptying recommendation.
- No single concentration is treated as a complete severity decision.
- No automatic dialysis/ECTR trigger or discharge decision.
- Poison-centre/medical-toxicology/critical-care consultation is emphasized for severe, unknown or high-risk exposures.
- Regional pesticide/envenomation pathways require local product/species/antivenom verification.

## Source backbone
AHA poisoning/special-circumstances guidance, WHO/IPCS poisoning and chemical-exposure resources, WHO pesticide and South-East Asia snakebite guidance, FDA acetylcysteine labeling, CDC carbon-monoxide/cyanide guidance, EXTRIP and current medicine labeling.
