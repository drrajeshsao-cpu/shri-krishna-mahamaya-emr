# V5.40W — Kidney/Renal Dose • AKI/CKD • Dialysis • TDM Intelligence Lab

Build: **V5.40W • MTLPI v2.4W • RENAL-PK/TDM v1.0 • R25W**

## Scope
Cross-cutting renal pharmacology workspace for medication decisions in stable CKD, changing AKI/recovery, extremes of body size, intermittent hemodialysis, CRRT, peritoneal dialysis, nephrotoxin burden and therapeutic drug monitoring.

## Dataset
- 44 renal/TDM classes
- 88 detailed medication/safety profiles
- Cumulative: 29 specialized labs • 921 classes • 2097 profiles

## Safety architecture
No automatic renal dose is generated. The workspace deliberately separates **kidney-function assessment**, **drug-specific renal metric**, **AKI/non-steady-state status**, **dialysis modality**, **TDM sample timing**, and **clinical action**. A flagged medicine is not automatically stopped; current product labeling, specialist/local protocol and patient-specific benefit-risk review remain mandatory.

## Evidence anchors
- KDIGO 2024 CKD guideline — drug dosing using validated eGFR in most stable settings; higher-accuracy GFR assessment when narrow therapeutic index/serious toxicity or creatinine-based estimates are unreliable.
- FDA renal-impairment pharmacokinetic guidance — effect of renal impairment on drug exposure and dosing information.
- ASHP/PIDS/SIDP/IDSA vancomycin monitoring guideline — AUC-guided monitoring framework for serious MRSA infection.
- FDA tobramycin labeling — renal/auditory/vestibular monitoring and renal impairment precautions.
- DailyMed/current product labeling for agent-specific thresholds and dialysis instructions.
