## Scope
Shock/resuscitation pharmacology, vasopressors/inotropes, RSI induction and neuromuscular blockade, ICU analgesia/sedation/delirium, ARDS paralysis/rescue pulmonary vasodilators, stress-ulcer and VTE prophylaxis, IV insulin, severe-acidemia bicarbonate context, de-resuscitation and selected titratable ICU infusions.

## Dataset
- 33 pharmacology/strategy classes
- 55 medicine/strategy profiles
- Cumulative: 24 specialized labs • 725 classes • 1711 profiles

## Safety architecture
ICU/critical-care research and learning CDS only. This lab is not an infusion calculator, RSI order set, ventilator protocol, or autonomous treatment engine. Drug choice, concentration, route, access, titration target, compatibility, organ function, hemodynamic phenotype, airway plan, monitoring and local ICU protocol must be verified by trained clinicians at the bedside.
