# Mahamaya Clinic EMR V5.54 — General Examination Intelligence Suite

**Build:** 2026.08.15-R31  
**Suite:** MGEIS-2.0  
**Base preserved:** V5.53 Clinical Examination Research Fast Flow / MCE-RP1 v1.0

## Purpose
V5.54 extends General Examination without turning it into a long duplicate of Systemic Examination. The design principle is:

**Quick screen → abnormal sign → focused phenotype → red flags → next bedside checks → clinician-review investigations → Systemic Examination when organ-specific detail is needed.**

The new modules are documentation and explainable clinical decision-support aids. They do not autonomously diagnose disease, order investigations, prescribe treatment, calculate prognosis, or replace emergency/specialist pathways.

## Final top-level workflow
- Core Exam
- Red Flags
- Oedema
- Gait
- Pain
- More Signs
- Interpret & Learn
- Research & QA

`More Signs` keeps the top bar compact and contains five focused submodules:
- Pallor / Anaemia
- Icterus
- Lymph Nodes
- Hydration / Perfusion
- Nutrition / Frailty / Functional Reserve

## 1. Vitals & Acute Red-Flag Intelligence
Captures the same six physiological domains that form the basis of NEWS2: respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, systolic blood pressure, pulse, consciousness/new confusion, and temperature. The app deliberately does **not** claim to replace the official NEWS2 chart, patient-specific oxygen targets, or local escalation policy.

Additional clinician-entered emergency features include chest-pain/ACS concern, severe dyspnoea, collapse/shock, new focal neurologic deficit, seizure/altered sensorium, major bleeding, glucose emergency concern and anaphylaxis/airway swelling.

The engine can surface urgent-review prompts but does not make autonomous disposition decisions.

Primary reference: Royal College of Physicians NEWS2.  
https://www.rcp.ac.uk/resources/national-early-warning-score-news-2/

## 2. Pallor / Anaemia Intelligence
Records pallor status, degree, observed sites, optional known haemoglobin, symptoms and major etiologic contexts. Clinical pallor is explicitly separated from laboratory diagnosis.

The first-line verification direction is CBC/haemoglobin, with morphology/iron/B12/folate/bleeding or other investigations suggested only according to documented context.

Primary reference: WHO 2024 guideline on haemoglobin cutoffs to define anaemia.  
https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240088542

## 3. Icterus / Hepatobiliary Intelligence
Captures scleral/skin icterus, onset, optional bilirubin value and associated features such as dark urine, pale stool, pruritus, RUQ pain, fever, organomegaly, ascites, encephalopathy, bleeding tendency and drug/herb/toxin exposure.

Outputs are direction-based (for example cholestatic/biliary, haemolytic or decompensation concern) rather than diagnostic labels.

## 4. Lymph Node Intelligence
Captures region, size, duration, consistency, mobility, tenderness, skin/sinus findings, generalized vs localized pattern, TB context, constitutional symptoms, hepatosplenomegaly and serious-pathology features.

When peripheral lymph-node TB is clinically suspected, the app points toward current national/WHO site-appropriate testing rather than diagnosing TB from node appearance.

Primary WHO references:
- Extrapulmonary TB diagnostic approaches: https://tbksp.who.int/en/node/2006
- WHO TB Knowledge Sharing: peripheral lymph-node TB is a recognized extrapulmonary presentation.

## 5. Hydration / Perfusion Intelligence
Captures mucosa, capillary refill, urine-output history, peripheral temperature, JVP/venous filling, orthostatic symptoms, fluid/weight trend and major intake/loss contributors.

No single physical sign is treated as definitive volume status. The panel is intended to support longitudinal clinical context and prompt urgent review for shock/altered sensorium/anuria-type concerns.

## 6. Nutrition / Frailty / Functional Reserve
Captures appetite, unintentional weight change, activity reserve, chair-rise ability, clinical muscle-strength impression, ADL dependence, optional validated-tool result and risk features such as muscle wasting, slow gait, falls, dysphagia, food-access problems and severe inability to maintain intake.

This panel is **not** a newly invented frailty score. If a formal score is required, the clinician records the validated instrument actually used.

WHO ICOPE recognizes mobility limitation and malnutrition among important intrinsic-capacity domains in older people.  
https://www.who.int/teams/maternal-newborn-child-adolescent-health-and-ageing/ageing-and-health/integrated-care-for-older-people-icope

## Data design
The new suite is stored inside:

`exam.intelligence.generalIntelligenceSuite`

This preserves the existing exam schema and V5.53 research metadata. Existing core General Examination statuses remain the source of truth for Pallor, Icterus, Lymphadenopathy and Dehydration; the focused modules synchronize those statuses rather than creating competing duplicate findings.

## Research / scientist design principles
- Raw observations stay separate from derived interpretation.
- “Not assessed” is different from “Absent”.
- Optional numerical values require source/unit verification.
- Investigation suggestions remain clinician-review actions.
- No invented probability, severity or frailty score is generated.
- Longitudinal repeat data can be retained with the patient visit record.
- Existing V5.53 Research & QA JSON export remains available; the new suite is nested in the examination intelligence object.

## Fast-flow routing
The existing `Next Focused` command now routes to the most relevant module when possible:
- Respiratory distress/cyanosis → Red Flags
- Pallor → Pallor/Anaemia
- Icterus → Icterus
- Lymphadenopathy → Lymph Nodes
- Dehydration → Hydration/Perfusion
- Oedema → Oedema
- Abnormal gait → Gait
- Pain > 0 → Pain
- Otherwise → Interpret & Learn

## Deliberately not duplicated as separate top-level tabs
Cyanosis, clubbing and respiratory distress remain in Core General Examination and are developed further in CVS/Respiratory Systemic Examination. This keeps General Examination fast and avoids turning it into another full systemic exam.

## Authentication boundary
V5.54 is a Clinical Examination UI/data/decision-support extension only. Login/PIN/Firebase authentication logic is intentionally outside this change.
