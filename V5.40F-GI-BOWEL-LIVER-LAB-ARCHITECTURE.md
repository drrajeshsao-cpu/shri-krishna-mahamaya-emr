# V5.40F — GI • Bowel • Liver Intelligence Lab

Build: 2026.08.14-R25F
MTLPI: v2.4F

## Scope
Adds a dedicated GI, bowel and liver researcher workspace on top of V5.40E.

- 40 pharmacology classes
- 80 detailed drug/formulation profiles
- Common GI: nausea/vomiting, gastroparesis, constipation, opioid constipation, acute/chronic diarrhea and ORS
- Disorders of gut-brain interaction: IBS-C, IBS-D, antispasmodics
- IBD: 5-ASA, corticosteroid induction, thiopurines, methotrexate, anti-TNF, anti-integrin, IL-23/IL-12-23, JAK and S1P therapies
- Pancreas: pancreatic enzyme replacement
- Liver: hepatic encephalopathy, ascites, portal hypertension, variceal vasoactive therapy, HRS, PBC, cholestatic pruritus, MASH/MASLD and severe alcohol-associated hepatitis
- Acid-peptic / H. pylori remains in the existing dedicated Acid-Peptic Lab.

## Safety boundary
IBD advanced therapies and decompensated-cirrhosis/HRS/variceal-bleeding therapies are specialist/high-risk. Exact diagnosis, severity, infection screening, formulation, dose, duration, organ function, pregnancy, interactions and current label/guideline must be verified. Obeticholic acid is excluded from active PBC options because the U.S. accelerated-approval PBC indication was withdrawn in 2025.
