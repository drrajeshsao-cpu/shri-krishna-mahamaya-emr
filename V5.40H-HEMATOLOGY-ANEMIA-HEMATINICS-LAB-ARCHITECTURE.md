# V5.40H — Hematology • Anemia • Hematinics Intelligence Lab

Build: 2026.08.14-R25H
MTLPI: v2.4H

## Scope
Adds a dedicated hematology/anemia/hematinics research workspace on top of V5.40G.

- 29 pharmacology/support classes
- 66 drug/formulation/blood-component profiles
- Iron deficiency: oral iron, newer oral formulations and IV iron complexes.
- Macrocytic anemia: B12 and folate pathways.
- CKD anemia: iron optimization, ESA and HIF-PHI learning layers aligned to KDIGO 2026.
- Sickle cell / thalassemia: disease-modifying therapy and iron chelation.
- ITP / thrombocytopenia: steroids, IVIG, TPO-RA, SYK and B-cell strategies.
- Neutropenia: G-CSF.
- Hemostasis/transfusion: antifibrinolytics, vitamin K, DDAVP, factor/fibrinogen support and RBC/platelet/plasma concepts.
- Sources: WHO, NHM Anaemia Mukt Bharat, NICE, KDIGO, ASH, NHLBI, ICMR, AABB and DailyMed.

## Safety boundary
This is research/learning CDS. Treat the cause of anemia, not the Hb value alone. Transfusion, IV iron, ESA/HIF-PHI, hemoglobinopathy therapy, ITP and factor products require current guideline/label plus patient-specific laboratory and specialist verification.
