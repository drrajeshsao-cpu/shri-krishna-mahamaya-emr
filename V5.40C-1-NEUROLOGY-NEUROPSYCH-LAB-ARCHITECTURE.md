## Purpose
Dedicated scientist-style learning workspace for common neurology and neuro-psychiatry pharmacology while preserving all prior Modern Therapeutics labs.

## Coverage
- Epilepsy/ASMs: focal, generalized, absence, rescue/status, reproductive-safety lens.
- Migraine/headache: acute triptans/gepants/ditan, prevention including CGRP mAbs/gepants, conventional preventives, chronic-migraine botulinum toxin, cluster headache.
- Vertigo/vestibular: short-term vestibular suppressants, antiemetics, betahistine context.
- Parkinson disease: levodopa, dopamine agonists, MAO-B, COMT, amantadine, anticholinergics, A2A antagonist.
- Dementia: AChEI, memantine, anti-amyloid mAbs with specialist/ARIA boundary.
- Stroke adjunct: antiplatelet and high-intensity statin secondary-prevention learning.
- Depression/anxiety: SSRIs, SNRIs, multimodal agents, TCAs, MAOIs, rapid-acting specialist agents, benzodiazepine and non-benzodiazepine anxiolytics.
- Insomnia: Z-drugs, melatonin pathway, orexin antagonists.
- Psychosis/bipolar: SGA/FGA/LAI antipsychotics, lithium and antiseizure mood stabilizers.

## Scientific boundaries
No universal first-line ranking is inferred from a class card. Dose text is an adult learning snapshot only. High-consequence areas — valproate reproductive safety, status epilepticus, lithium, clozapine, benzodiazepines, anti-amyloid mAbs, MAOIs and depot antipsychotics — require current label/guideline verification and condition-specific monitoring.
