SCIENTIFIC BOUNDARIES

- Adult dose fields are learning/reference snapshots and require current label/guideline + patient-specific verification.
- Detailed renal/hepatic/pregnancy/pediatric/elderly/interaction/QT/bleeding-duration safety enrichment is intentionally deferred to the next dedicated safety phase.
- Pharma/manufacturer promotional pages are not used to rank medicines; manufacturer-specific claims must be checked against regulator-approved product information.
